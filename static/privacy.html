Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Viavios is a place to write your memoir, legacy book, chronicle, testament, journal, or photo story. Your life story belongs to you. This policy explains, in plain language, what we collect and what we never do.

What we collect

Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password (or your sign-in provider, such as Google, and passkeys you register). We never see or store your Google password.

Your writing and media — the books, chapters, photos, recordings, timeline entries, and letters you create. They are stored so you can read, edit, share, and print them.

Activity needed to run the service — your plan, token balance and token history, votes on public stories, and the record of emails the service creates for you (welcome, receipts, account updates).

What we never do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not sell or share your writing. Your private books stay private; a book is visible to others only when you choose Family or Public sharing, or through the testament settings you control.

Writing helpers and your words

Some writing helpers (grammar check, AI suggestions) run only when you click them. When you click, the passage you are working on is sent to the checking service for that single check or suggestion. Nothing is sent while you simply write, and passages sent for a check are not used to train models by us.

Email

We send account emails — welcome, receipts, account updates, and service news. Stored copies appear in your Account page.

Payments

Checkout on this demo is simulated; no card is charged. When live payments begin, card details are handled by the payment provider and never touch our servers.

Deleting your information

You can close your account at any time from the Account page. Closing deletes your books, chapters, photos, letters, and sign-in credentials. A confirmation email is created for the address on the account.

Children

Viavios is for adults. Accounts are created by the account holder; gift purchases are made by adults for adults.

Contact

Questions about privacy: write to the address on your account emails, or the support contact listed on the Viavios home page.
